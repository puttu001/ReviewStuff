# ReviewStuff

ReviewStuff helps you revisit useful links, ideas, and notes instead of letting them disappear into bookmarks and saved-post lists.

## Problem statement

We often find something valuable, save it, and then forget about it. That information is only useful if we return to it at the right time. ReviewStuff solves this by keeping saved content organized and bringing it back through a simple daily review flow.

## Features

- Save links or text notes manually.
- Share content directly to ReviewStuff from other mobile apps.
- Automatically fetch useful link details such as the title, image, website name, and favicon.
- Organize saved items by topic.
- Filter the library by topic or content source.
- Review due items through a focused daily review queue.
- Mark items as reviewed or skip them for later.
- Secure each library with Google Sign-In.
- View the signed-in Google account and sign out safely from the account screen.
- Choose between light and dark themes.
- Install ReviewStuff on a phone as a Progressive Web App (PWA).
- Queue shared saves in the background when the connection is slow or temporarily unavailable.

## Example use cases

- Save an article and revisit it when you have time to read it properly.
- Keep educational videos, tutorials, and documentation together by topic.
- Capture an idea or short note before it is forgotten.
- Build a personal learning library from content shared across different apps.
- Regularly review research, career resources, recipes, travel ideas, or anything worth remembering.

## Install on your phone

Open [ReviewStuff](https://reviewstuff.pankajk.dev) on your phone, then follow the instructions for your device.

### Android

1. Open the link in Google Chrome.
2. Open the Chrome menu.
3. Select **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.
4. Confirm by tapping **Install**.

### iPhone or iPad

1. Open the link in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Scroll down and select **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

ReviewStuff will then appear on your home screen and can be opened like a regular app.
